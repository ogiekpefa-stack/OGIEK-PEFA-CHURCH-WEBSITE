About Ogiek PEFA Church

Ogiek PEFA Church is a Christian church located in Tinet Ward, Kuresoi South Constituency, Nakuru County, Kenya.

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centered church that transforms lives through the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel, make disciples, strengthen families, empower young people and serve the community.
